# SPUZL launch copy

Ready-to-paste text for listings and posts. Every claim here matches the live site: the FAQ on
https://spuzl.vercel.app, `/how-to-play`, `/llms.txt` and `lib/copy/seo.ts`. If a feature changes, update this file
too.

Facts to keep consistent everywhere:

- Name: **SPUZL**. Link: https://spuzl.vercel.app
- Maker: Senya (https://wisdomska.site). Made in Ghana.
- 29 free games in 4 worlds:
  - Puzzles (9): Crownfall, Dusk, Sixer, Nine, Weave, Flowline, Pairup, Lost, Trotro Jam.
  - Words & Quiz (9): Coil, Rungs, Quads, Seven, Minicross, Buried, Remix, Quickfire, Emoji Hits.
  - Arcade (6): Echo, Twins, Flinch, Beat Tap, Gotcha, Dash.
  - Board Games (5): Chess, Draughts, Oware, Ludo, Four Up.
- Runs in the browser on phones, tablets and computers. Add it to the home screen and it opens like an app. A
  puzzle already on screen can be finished offline.
- Free. No download, no ads, nothing to buy with money. Sparks are earned by playing and buy looks for Spuz, the
  mascot. Nobody can pay to win.
- Play as a guest after giving a birth month and year. Signing in (6-digit email code or Google) is optional and
  keeps progress across phones.
- Play with friends:
  - Duel: the same puzzle, best of three.
  - Party: a room code, up to 8 phones racing at once.
  - Board games against a friend or the bot.
  - Weekly leagues of 30 players.
  - The Daily Five: five quick games a day.
- Ages 13 and older. For 13 to 15:
  - quick preset chat only;
  - friends by invite link or QR code;
  - Quick Match only with other 13 to 15 players;
  - city and country never shown.
- Fair play: every clear is checked on the server.

Do not claim: download counts, user numbers, ratings or reviews, an app store app, or that SPUZL is "the first" or
"the best" anything.
